description=[h2]What it is for[/h2]
description=RCON alone tells an admin very little. With this mod on the server, SpiffoCON can also:
description=[list]
description=[*]show the players on the game's map, with their health, vehicle, the zombies near them and where players died;
description=[*]open a player's sheet: health part by part, needs, traits, skills with their experience, what they wear and carry;
description=[*]heal a player, cure a zombie infection, take hunger, thirst and tiredness away, give or take a trait, set a skill level, set hair and beard;
description=[*]repair, clean, fill or recharge the items a player carries, or remove them;
description=[*]show a vehicle part by part and repair it (missing wheels and windows are put back), refuel it, give its key;
description=[*]list the animals near the players, with which ones are pregnant, feed and water them, fill their troughs;
description=[*]put right the buckets, bottles, troughs and rain collectors that can't hold liquids any more, and the drying racks, workbenches and other stations that can't be used any more, after a server crash; when switched on, also by itself every ten minutes, with SpiffoCON closed too;
description=[*]clean up around a spot: zombie corpses, items on the ground, fires, wrecks;
description=[*]set the weather and the time of day, put items on the ground, list and remove safehouses;
description=[*]write in the chat as an ordinary line, not as an alert in the middle of the screen, also to one player alone;
description=[*]remove an object of the world that nobody can pick up or dismantle any more;
description=[*]list the generators near the players, refuel, repair and switch them;
description=[*]show how the server is doing: its updates second by second, to tell a lag of the server from one of a player's PC.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]How to use it[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*]Add the mod to the server: Mods=SpiffoCONBridge and WorkshopItems=3809683986, then restart it.
description=[*]In SpiffoCON, set the server's SFTP access: the bridge connects by itself.
description=[/olist]
description=
description=It works on the server. On a player's game it does one thing: it shows the chat lines an admin writes from SpiffoCON. It acts only on requests written into the server's Zomboid/Lua folder, so only someone who can already write the server's files can use it. Every admin action is written to the server's admin log.
